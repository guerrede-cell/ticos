Common questions

What is the difference between quartz, granite and marble?

Quartz is engineered from natural quartz and resin, so it comes in many colors and needs little upkeep. Granite is a natural stone known for resisting heat and scratches. Marble is prized for its veining and elegant, one-of-a-kind patterns.

Which stone is right for my project?

It depends on your style, budget and how you use the space. Granite is great for kitchens, marble suits bathrooms and decorative pieces, and quartz works almost anywhere. We will walk you through the options at the showroom.

How much do countertops cost?

Marble is usually the most expensive option, while quartz and granite are generally more affordable. Call us or send the form for a free estimate on your project.

How long does installation take?

Most installations take a few days to a week, depending on the size and complexity of the project.

Can I see samples before I choose?

Yes. Visit our Bridgewater showroom to see samples and full slabs in person.
